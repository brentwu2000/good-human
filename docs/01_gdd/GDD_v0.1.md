# GOOD HUMAN! — GDD v0.1

## Core Fantasy
You are a dog raising and training your human. You explore a familiar neighborhood, search for useful objects, influence your human through dog behavior, confront other dog-human pairs, and extract before losing valuable finds.

## Run Loop
Home → configure → walk → search → dog behavior/training → encounter → human auto battle + dog intervention → loot/territory → greed vs extract → bank progress → repeat.

## Human
One random owner per save. Core stats begin with STR, END, AGI, WIL (SOC can serve events later). Growth comes from TrainingTags such as RUN, STRAIN, COURAGE, SOCIAL and ENDURE. Combat uses equipped skills and autonomous AI.

## Dog
Dog progression is distinct: smell, courage, intimidation, charm/social, agility and territorial instinct. Dog backpack provides a small number of safe slots.

## Inventory Risk
Three layers: Human Run Inventory (at risk), Dog Safe Inventory (survives defeat), Home Stash (banked).

## World
Fixed neighborhood/park map with semi-random content. Residential areas are safer; park center is higher risk/reward. Extraction begins becoming available after ~5 minutes.

## Combat (later sprint)
Human fights autonomously. Dog QTEs are low-frequency and identity-specific, initially leash pull and bark.

## Defeat (later sprint)
Human is hospitalized, unprotected run loot and carried human equipment are lost, dog safe slots and permanent progression remain.

## Territory (later sprint)
Dogs claim special locations by marking them. Initial MVP eventually needs only one simplified territory point.

## Narrative
Light optional story. First chapter culminates in challenging the park king.
