# Phase 2 — Character Art Brief

## Objective
Produce the first character-quality vertical slice for GOOD HUMAN!.

Do not mass-produce characters yet. Establish a visual standard first.

## Deliverables

### ART-010 — Player Dog Visual Target
A production-quality target for the player dog.

Must read clearly at mobile gameplay scale.

### ART-011 — Owner Visual Target
Modern ordinary human with enough facial/body readability to support attachment and later visual evolution.

### ART-012 — Opponent Dog
A distinct dog that remains readable beside its owner.

### ART-013 — Ordinary Opponent Human
Modern urban appearance.

### ART-014 — Old Master
High priority identity piece.

Requirements:
- looks older, ordinary, possibly frail;
- must NOT visually scream “martial arts master”;
- paired with a relatively unthreatening dog;
- combat pose/animation can reveal surprising competence only after engagement.

### ART-015 — Dog/Human Scale Guide
Define:
- gameplay sprite scale;
- dog-to-human proportion;
- leash visibility;
- collision/selection visual assumptions;
- camera-safe readable sizes.

### ART-016 — Core Movement
At minimum:
- Dog Idle
- Dog Walk
- Dog Run
- Dog Sniff
- Human Idle
- Human Walk

### ART-017 — Combat Pose Study
Minimum pose language for:
- neutral combat stance
- punch
- kick
- block
- dodge
- hit reaction
- defeat

### ART-018 — Gameplay Target 02
One polished target frame:
- player dog + owner;
- opponent dog + human;
- park/neighborhood encounter;
- readable mobile framing;
- modern urban GOOD HUMAN! visual identity.

## Quality Rule
Dog and owner are not allowed to ship as obvious generic free-pack characters.

A-grade free bases may be used for structure/animation, but final target must be transformed into a unified GOOD HUMAN! identity.
