# Sprint 05 — Greed Moment Storyboard v0.1

Status: REVIEW
Deliverable: D5-04
Reference: `assets/_source/design/territory/greed_moment_storyboard_01.png`

## Four-beat sequence

1. Safe exit — the bus stop is close, calm and clearly usable. The dog and owner are already oriented near homeward safety.
2. Value carried — the owner holds a visibly full ordinary bag. The dog notices the park while the owner looks back, establishing that something can be lost without using a warning overlay.
3. Temptation — a single black rival dog with coral neckerchief appears near Big Banyan Tree. A low violet scent trail connects the opportunity to the player's nose.
4. Voluntary choice — the player dog leans toward the scent and pulls the teal leash taut. The owner hesitates; the safe bus stop remains visible behind them. The frame does not resolve whether the player stays or extracts.

## Selected principles

- Keep the safe exit and temptation visible in the same composition whenever possible.
- The dog authors the decision through head direction, body lean and leash tension.
- Use warm late-afternoon light for emotional tension, not a red danger grade.
- Show value through the owner's carried bag and the Risk HUD, never floating loot totals in the world.
- One persistent rival reads more strongly than a crowd of anonymous opponents.

## Runtime handoff

The sequence can be assembled from existing systems: extraction availability, `RunValue`, Risk HUD, active scent cue, Banyan landmark, rival presence, dog motion and leash tension. No cutscene or separate territory battle scene is required.
