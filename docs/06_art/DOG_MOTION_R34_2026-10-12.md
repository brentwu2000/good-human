# Dog motion contact polish r34 — 2026-10-12

Resumed and completed the unfinished r34 work. Published eight dog Mesh2Motion
libraries and the Chihuahua cheek texture repair. Status: REVIEW / grade B.
This closes the r34 contact-polish pass, not all remaining character-art work.

## Changes

- Bake dog motion at 120 Hz and retain that rate in Godot's animation import.
  Run measures actual skin at quarter-frame intervals and lifts neighboring
  keys by the measured deficit plus 0.1 mm. Linear keys avoid overshoot.
- Reduce Run limb rotation limits from 36 to 24 degrees for Chihuahua and Poodle;
  the smooth angular response retains movement while reducing local stretching.
- Put the previously authored r30 Ucupaint cheek texture onto the r33 Chihuahua
  skin. Duplicate side mouths are removed; geometry, UVs, normals, weights,
  bone names, original animations and their binary data remain unchanged.
- Preserve all nine M2M names and durations. Human animation behavior is unchanged.

No new third-party assets. Existing Mesh2Motion and model provenance applies;
the texture derives from the existing r30 work.

## Validation

Seven required suites passed, **346 assertions**: breed_models 81, dog_motion 80,
owner_gesture 14, weapon 103, weapon_clip 52, weapon_fight_contact 8,
fight_body_contact 8. Godot 4.6.3 was used. The weapon test still prints
dummy-renderer null-material/resource-leak errors; fight_body_contact prints an
exit leak warning. Assertions pass and processes exit 0. Logs are retained;
this is not represented as a warning-free run.

Fresh Godot captures: 720 images covering eight dogs, nine clips, five phases,
before/after. All nine after contact sheets and focused comparisons reviewed.
A further ten images compare r33 with the published Chihuahua Run loaded from
runtime paths. Capture logs contain no rendering/script errors.

Independent skin evaluation uses 17 poses per clip and **209 samples per Run**,
including between-key positions. Worst sampled ground penetration is below
0.001 mm after correction (floating-point scale). This is sampled evidence,
not a proof for every possible blended runtime pose.

## Remaining limitations

Inherited meshes still show angular silhouettes, facial/material seams,
distorted limbs and seated-body constraints, especially Chihuahua/Poodle.
Fetch/Alert/Bark are subtle; no jaw bone was added. Original embedded Sit is
unchanged. Foot sliding, contact during animation blending and mobile performance
of denser libraries are not certified. These are B-grade repairs, not S-grade
final characters. The rejected local-paw weight trial was not published.

Other handoff work remains separate: CX-01 umbrella, CX-02 impact-effect choice,
CX-03 jaw rigs, CX-05 owner clips, CX-07 Poodle budget, and optional CX-08/09.
