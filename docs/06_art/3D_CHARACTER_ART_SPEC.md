# GOOD HUMAN! — 3D Character Art Spec v0.1

Status: REVIEW for the playable prototype  
Target: P-02 dog-height chase camera, 1 unit = 1 metre

## Readability targets

- Player dog body length: about 0.72 m, visible height about 0.85 m including ears.
- Owner visible height: about 1.9 m, with head, hair, clothing, backpack and hands readable from behind.
- Dog is the visual anchor; owner remains clearly ordinary and must not telegraph combat power.
- The leash begins at the owner hand height and terminates at the dog collar/chest harness.
- Silhouettes must read at the camera’s default dog-view distance before material detail is added.

## Prototype construction

- Low-poly `CapsuleMesh`, `SphereMesh`, `CylinderMesh`, and `BoxMesh` pieces are generated by `world/greybox/greybox.gd`.
- Dog: elongated body, head/muzzle, ears, eyes, four legs, tail, and teal chest harness.
- Owner: separate legs, torso, arms, head, hair volume, and small backpack.
- Encounter colors remain data-driven through the existing shirt/pants/hair parameters.
- Materials are matte, non-metallic, and intentionally simple for the mobile renderer.

## Upgrade path

This prototype is the approved proportion and silhouette baseline, not the final S-level model. Replace each generated visual root with imported GLB/scene assets only after confirming camera framing, leash anchors, owner fade, and combat readability in the 3D slice.
