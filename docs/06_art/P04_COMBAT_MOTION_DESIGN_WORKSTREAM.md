# P-04 Design / Codex ART

D4-18 Human Combat Motion Language:
Combat Idle, Approach, Circle, Backstep, Jab, Heavy Hook, Kick, Block, Dodge, Hit Light, Hit Heavy, Stumble, Down.
Prioritize readable/exaggerated silhouettes, windups and misses; no generic forward/backward lean.

D4-19 Footwork Board:
6–10 second exchange with distance/angle changes.

D4-20 Contact / Impact:
Jab contact, heavy contact, block, missed heavy strike, stumble.

D4-21 Dog Physical Presence:
dog blocked by leg, slides around human, retreating human approaches dog, dog circles behind opponent, dog crosses fighter path.

D4-22 Dog POV Brawl Composition:
verify readability as physical bodies move toward/away from camera.

D4-23 Combat Personality Seeds:
visual exploration only — UNTRAINED / SCRAPPER / CALM.

Append; never overwrite previous D4 work.
