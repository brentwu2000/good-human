# Sprint 04 — DESIGN WORKSTREAM

Design starts immediately, in parallel with P-01 Camera engineering.

Owner: Design / Art Direction (Codex may execute visual exploration).
Engineering implementation is blocked until Camera Gate, but design deliverables are not.

## D-01 Dog Agency Interaction Map
Produce one diagram showing dog, owner, opponent human, opponent dog, leash, Bark influence, Pull influence, nearby interactables and disengage.

## D-02 Bark Visual Language
Explore how Bark is communicated without a large skill button:
- body pose
- mouth/head animation
- subtle world feedback
- attention reaction
- cooldown/resistance feedback
Deliver 3 visual directions, choose 1 candidate.

## D-03 Leash Visual Language
Define:
- slack
- light tension
- strong tension
- dangerous/max tension
- pull direction readability
- owner stumble/recovery
Must work at mobile size.

## D-04 Dog Combat Readability
Create representative frame(s):
human vs human fight while player dog remains foreground/nearby.
Need to read who owns whom, leash relationship, threats and interactables.

Prepare both:
- top-down adaptation
- Dog Eye/Hybrid adaptation
until P-01 decides.

## D-05 Opponent Dog Reactions
Minimum pose/behavior sheet:
neutral, watching, bark-back, retreat/fear, sniff/social, excited/chaotic.

## D-06 Owner Reaction Sheet
Minimum:
follow, leash pulled, stumble, recover, dodge-by-pull, ignore dog, react to bark.

## D-07 Mobile HUD Exploration
Do NOT design conventional RPG combat bar.
Explore minimal contextual controls for Bark/Interact and movement.
Create variants for A and B/C camera paths.

## D-08 Target Screenshot 03 — Dog Agency
A polished composition/reference, not necessarily production asset:
player dog actively affecting a seamless human fight.
Must visually communicate GOOD HUMAN! without explanatory text.

## D-09 Smell/Search Compatibility
Check that Dog Agency HUD does not conflict with future smell/search visual language.

## D-10 Design Decision Pack
After Camera Gate, consolidate selected camera + Bark + Leash + HUD into one implementation-ready sheet.
