## Decisions represented

- Dog-height chase camera remains the accepted camera family.
- Safety and temptation coexist in one frame; neither requires a cutscene.
- Greed is voluntary and authored by the dog, not imposed by a red warning state.
- The owner carries visible ordinary value but does not display combat power.
- Territory is rooted in a memorable place and rival relationship, not a flag or capture ring.
- The scent path stays close to nose height and does not resemble a human quest arrow.
