# CX-01 umbrella readability — 2026-10-12

Published to `assets/props/weapons/umbrella.glb`. CX-01 readability pass complete;
art remains REVIEW / grade B, not S/final-quality approval.

The dark navy canopy now uses muted warm ochre with the retained navy closure
strap and metallic ferrule. A honey-colored compact J-hook replaces the straight
black butt. The canopy diameter is unchanged; contrast carries the improvement
at 405×720 without enlarging the striking end.

## Geometry and integration contract

- Grip stays at the origin; glTF +Y remains the striking direction.
- Tip stays at +0.600 m. `WeaponProp3D.TIP` and reach calibration need no edit.
- Rear end changes from -0.100 to -0.080 m; total length is now 0.680 m.
- Main grip at the fist retains its original radius. J-hook outer X extent is
  0.042 m, within the canopy's 0.052 m radius. Overall X/Z bounds stay ±0.052 m.
- 1,744 triangles, up from 1,028; four plain PBR materials, no image textures.
- No gameplay poses, grip JSON, weapons data or collision tolerances changed.

An initial 0.074 m-wide hook was rejected: the measured weapon capsule overlapped
the holder's torso by 61 mm during Thrust. The final compact hook reduces that
measurement to 13 mm, below the existing 30 mm allowance. This is tolerance-based
validation, not a claim of mathematically zero intersections.

## Validation and visual review

Godot **4.6.3**, Movie Maker, **405×720 at 30 FPS**:

- Weapon showcase: 881 frames / 29.37 s, including carry walk/idle, guard,
  steps, thrust, swing, block, dodge and reactions.
- Real fight: 405 frames / 13.50 s, using the existing `brawl_capture` umbrella
  case, the same case invoked by `record.sh umbrella`.
- Both before and final after recordings completed. Reviewed 29 showcase and
  13 fight stills from the final recordings, plus the original baseline images.
- The recording scenes inherit the existing capture scenes and add only a still
  recorder; gameplay/capture choreography is unchanged. Opponent clothing and
  fight progression can differ across runs, so comparisons are visual context,
  not pixel-identical experiments.
- Seven required suites pass, **346 assertions**: breed_models 81, dog_motion 80,
  owner_gesture 14, weapon 103, weapon_clip 52, weapon_fight_contact 8,
  fight_body_contact 8. Weapon and capture processes retain exit resource-leak
  warnings; weapon_test also reports dummy-renderer null-material errors.
  These messages are retained in the logs; all suites exit 0.

Blender 5.2 bridge inspection confirmed the hook shape. The repeatable builder
was exercised through the bridge after replacing its factory-reset operation;
it completed and the bridge remained connected.

## Remaining limits and provenance

The compact hook reads in close views, but the hand can obscure it and the hole
is only a few pixels at mobile resolution. Canopy contrast is the principal
readability improvement. Cloth texture, wear and more convincing pleats remain
future art work; this is still B-grade authored prop art.
