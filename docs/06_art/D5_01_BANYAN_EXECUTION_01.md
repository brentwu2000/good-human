## Geometry and material handoff

Keep the three major trunk volumes legible before adding bark relief. Use irregular taper and merging seams rather than three perfect cylinders. Ground roots should appear embedded in soil, with credible contact shadows. Build the approach from the actual navigable terrain; a picturesque root must not become an accidental barrier or a hidden trip mechanic.

Preserve a clear path to the existing interaction position and a continuous bypass around the root zone. Check actual dog footprint, owner follow clearance and rival staging before finalizing geometry; this art document supplies no new collision dimensions or navigation rules.

Use muted bark, soil and leaf values that remain legible in warm sun and overcast light. Pale scar is a material variation, not a glowing quest marker. Aerial roots sit mainly above/outside the approach so thin strands do not obscure dog, leash or interaction. No flags, crowns, capture rings or faction canopy tint.

The reference is not a polygon/texture budget. Establish final budgets against the scene's mobile frame cost. Spend detail at dog-height bark and root intersections; simplify distant crown interior. Any LOD must preserve fused trunk silhouette, scar and principal roots; do not swap the landmark into an ordinary tree at distance.
