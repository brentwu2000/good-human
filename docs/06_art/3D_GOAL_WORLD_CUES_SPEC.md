# GOOD HUMAN! — 3D Goal World Cues v0.1

Status: REVIEW
Deliverable: ART-041

## Strange scent

Three low violet wisps drift, rotate and breathe close to the ground. They appear only while an active dog desire targets that cue. The effect is intentionally nose-height and does not resemble a human quest marker.

## Squirrel

The squirrel now has a readable body, head, ears, eyes, feet and three-volume upright tail. Its existing idle hop, flee direction and treed height animate the new visual without changing chase rules.

## Discovery presentation

New discoveries continue to use the existing Desire HUD popup and collection state. No additional full-screen overlay was added: world cues should invite the dog's attention rather than interrupt the walk or become a checklist.

Acceptance requires scent visibility at normal dog-view distance, a recognizable squirrel silhouette during movement, and unchanged goal progression, chase distances and save behavior.
