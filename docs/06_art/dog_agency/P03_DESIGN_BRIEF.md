# P-03 Design Brief — Street Brawl

Target: one memorable 30–60 second fight, not a library of combat content.

Visual thesis:
Explore as the dog. Fight through the dog's eyes.

Design must emphasize scale: humans should feel large from dog-eye height. Avoid conventional RPG combat framing, floating damage numbers and oversized skill bars.

Deliverables:
1. Explore→Tension→POV transition frames.
2. POV compositions for front/left/right/back/flank positions.
3. Punch/Kick/Block/Dodge readable silhouettes.
4. Light/heavy hit reactions and Down.
5. leash slack/tension/max tension in POV.
6. Bark reaction with opponent looking toward camera/dog.
7. owner crisis from low dog angle.
8. win pet/acknowledgement and defeat approach-owner beat.
9. ambience/world reaction callouts.
10. implementation capture markup.

Use `P03_STREET_BRAWL_STORYBOARD.png` as concept reference, not a pixel-perfect target.
