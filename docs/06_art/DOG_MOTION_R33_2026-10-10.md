# CX-04 dog deformation repair r33 — 2026-10-10

Published to the game's seven breed models and eight Mesh2Motion libraries.
This is a grade B deformation repair, not S-grade final character art.
Owner request: repair the dogs after Claude added Mesh2Motion; follow
`CLAUDE_HANDOFF_TO_CODEX_2026-10-09.md`, CX-04.

## What changed

1. Smoothed inherited skin-weight transitions on the seven breed models and
   retained eight influences per vertex. Rest geometry, indices, normals, UVs,
   textures, materials, bind rig, bone names and original Idle/Walk/Sit animation
   data are preserved. The player's original `shiba_01` skin is unchanged.
2. Calibrated virtual front-leg pivots against the actual front-paw positions.
   The original Chihuahua front-leg heads were behind the visible front legs.
   Equivalent pivot corrections are baked as bone translation tracks, keeping
   the existing bind skeleton and names compatible with gameplay.
3. Added smooth world-space rotation limits for the seven inherited breed
   skins, especially the extreme fox leg bends. World-space limits preserve
   parent/head compensation. `shiba_01` retains its source rotation amplitudes.
4. Grounded clips using the evaluated skinned surface. Small Run flight and
   Jump airtime remain; correcting only paw-bone markers had missed visible
   surface penetration. Blender's unskinned importer control Icosphere is
   explicitly excluded from bounds.

The runtime model and animation changes are paired. No gameplay GDScript or
human/weapon assets changed. No jaw bone was added (CX-03 remains separate).

## Remaining limitations

The severe tearing/ground disappearance is substantially reduced. This is not
a full anatomical re-rig: local stretching remains, particularly Chihuahua Run.
The original seated meshes still constrain gait quality. Facial projection
seams, duplicate mouths, existing geometry flaws, missing jaw articulation and
triangle budgets were not repaired by changing skin weights. Original embedded
Sit clips are preserved, not replaced by the M2M Sit shown here.

Between baked frames, measured surface penetration can still reach about
12 mm on Golden Run (other breeds less); foot contact and sliding need further
animation polish. Eight influences increase skinning cost; mobile performance
has not been profiled. Do not mark the dogs S-grade based on passing tests.
