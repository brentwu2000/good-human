# Sprint 03 — TRAIN
## Goal
Dog behavior → Training Event → TrainingTags → run resolution → persistent human Growth → visible change.
## Scope
Five tags: RUN, STRAIN, COURAGE, SOCIAL, ENDURE. Run-local accumulation, extraction conversion, partial defeat conversion, ~8 prototype perks, training summary, 3+ observable growth effects. No full skill tree, training minigame, or dog combat QTE.
## Rules
Training occurs in the normal Run World. Do not show raw `RUN +1` as primary feedback; describe the experience. Successful extraction starts at 100% conversion and defeat around 50%, both data-driven.
## Acceptance
5+ meaningful behaviors generate tags; tags resolve correctly; defeat preserves partial learning; growth persists; 3 effects are perceptible without Stats; players can intentionally alter dog behavior to influence development; WALK and seamless FIGHT remain functional; blind QA passes.
