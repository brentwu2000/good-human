# P-03 — STREET BRAWL VERTICAL SLICE

## Purpose
Build one polished 30–60 second encounter that proves GOOD HUMAN!'s combat identity before Sprint 05 engineering.

Core principle:
> Explore as the dog. Fight through the dog's eyes.

P-03 replaces text-only combat as the acceptance target. Combat remains seamless in the Run World.

## Player Experience
1. EXPLORE — third-person dog-height chase camera; dog visible.
2. TENSION — rival pair noticed; dogs stare/growl; owners react; ambience ducks.
3. CONFRONTATION — dogs provoke; leashes tighten; humans approach.
4. COMBAT SNAP — camera transitions into dog-eye first person without a scene cut.
5. ACTIVE FIGHT — camera auto-frames the human fight while dog movement remains active.
6. DOG INTERVENTION — Bark, flank, leash pull and nearby interaction remain available.
7. IMPACT — visible attacks, hit reactions, knockback, sound and restrained camera impulse.
8. CRISIS — owner condition becomes visibly worse; dog breathing/instinct increases tension.
9. RESOLUTION — opponent or owner goes down in the same world.
10. AFFECTION — owner may look at/pet/acknowledge dog; defeat allows dog to approach owner.
11. RETURN — camera smoothly pulls back to third person and walking resumes.

## Non-negotiable
- No BattleScene.
- No loading/teleport.
- No combat text as the primary presentation.
- No stationary humans exchanging hidden damage.
- No FPS free-look requirement on mobile.
- Player remains the dog throughout combat.
- Dog locomotion continues in first person.
- Camera automatically keeps the central fight readable.

## Prototype Scope
One owner, one opponent human, one opponent dog, one small street/park combat space.
Attacks: Punch, Kick.
Defense: Block, Dodge.
Reactions: Light Hit, Heavy Hit/Stagger, Down.
Dog actions: Bark, Leash Pull.
World reaction: minimum 1 NPC reaction + ambient dog/bird reaction.

## Acceptance
A first-time player should understand who is attacking, when a hit lands, when the owner is in danger, how Bark/Pull affected the exchange, and whether the fight ended — without reading combat log text.
