# ADR-015 — Dog POV Combat Camera
Status: PROPOSED / P-03 CANDIDATE

Explore uses dog-height third-person with the dog visible. During established combat, camera transitions seamlessly into dog-eye first-person while continuing to follow dog position and automatically framing the central human fight. Resolution transitions back to third-person.

Rationale: strengthen dog identity, human scale, tension, Bark/Pull immediacy and owner emotional connection.

P-02 owner-focused third-person remains the comparison baseline until P-03 QA. Final adoption requires playtest evidence and comfort/readability review.
