# ADR-008 — Behavior-Driven Human Training
## Status
ACCEPTED
## Decision
Human growth originates primarily from meaningful dog behavior/world events during normal runs. Training is not a separate minigame or conventional XP-allocation screen.
## Consequences
World actions emit semantic Training Events; TrainingTags are primarily internal; UI describes experiences; run training resolves into persistent growth; failure preserves partial learning; growth must include observable behavioral effects.
