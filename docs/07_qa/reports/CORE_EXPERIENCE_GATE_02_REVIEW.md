# Core Experience Gate 02 — Review

Date: 2026-09-18
Reviewer: owner playtest (Windows debug build after Sprint 04 + the 3D art pass,
camera turning fix and dog-agency balance pass). Codex blind QA not run.

## Test
Without relying on title/logo/lore, can a player describe the gameplay roughly as
"I am a dog, I train a human, the human fights for me, and I can run around and
interfere"?

## Evidence (owner)
Owner: "沒有明顯" — the identity does not come across.

All four evidence pillars asked about were reported weak:

| Pillar | Owner's read |
|---|---|
| TRAIN | The human's growth does not read as something the dog caused |
| DOG AGENCY | Combat still feels like spectating |
| GOALS | No reason to want another walk |
| WALK / FIGHT | Not strong enough on their own |

This is continuous with Core Loop Gate 01 (2026-09-17), which recorded
"有點沒感" on training and "節奏問題 遊戲感覺不有趣" on the loop, and was
passed as PROCEED WITH CHANGES with those items explicitly left open. They were
never closed; four sprints of content were built on top of them.

## Decision
REWORK CORE EXPERIENCE.

Sprint 05 GREED / TERRITORY engineering is paused at P4-010. P4-001 through
P4-010 stay REVIEW and are not reverted — they are unverified, not known-bad,
and the territory work is deliberately additive.

## Engineering note
Four pillars failing together points at something they share rather than four
separate system faults. The diagnosis and the rework options are to be agreed
with the owner before any rework is implemented; no core rules have been changed
on the strength of this gate alone.
