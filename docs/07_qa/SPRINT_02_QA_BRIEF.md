# Sprint 02 — Blind QA Brief

Use a fresh Codex context.

Do not inspect source code, Claude notes, prior QA reports or known bugs before Phase A.

## What You Know
- You play as the dog.
- Your human can fight other humans.
- You should use only controls/options exposed by the game.

## Phase A — Blind Exploration
Try several runs naturally.

Pay special attention to:
1. Did you understand that the human fights automatically?
2. Did you try to directly control the human?
3. Was watching combat interesting or did it feel passive?
4. Could you understand differences between Punch/Kick/Block/Dodge through behavior?
5. Did choosing Provoke feel meaningfully different from Leave?
6. Did you care about losing carried loot?
7. Did protected dog storage matter after defeat?
8. Did any opponent surprise you?
9. Did visual appearance cause you to misjudge strength?
10. During combat, did you wish the dog could do something? What did you naturally try?

Write the exploratory report BEFORE reading acceptance criteria.

## Phase B — Acceptance
Then validate the Sprint 02 Golden Path and explicit acceptance requirements in `SPRINT_02_FIGHT.md`.

Do not fix code while acting as QA.
