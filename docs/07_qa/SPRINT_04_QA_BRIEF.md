# Sprint 04 Blind QA Brief

Fresh Codex QA context.

Primary question:
While humans fight autonomously, did you feel you had meaningful things to do as the dog?

Observe:
- what you naturally tried before learning controls;
- whether Bark consequence was understandable;
- whether Leash Pull felt spatial rather than like a QTE button;
- whether you discovered useful/harmful timing;
- whether you moved around the fight;
- whether you interacted with nearby world objects;
- whether dog actions felt funny/systemic rather than scripted;
- whether dog actions connected to TRAIN;
- whether combat remained readable in the selected camera;
- whether you ever felt reduced to spectator.

Do not inspect source or exact hidden thresholds before exploratory notes.
