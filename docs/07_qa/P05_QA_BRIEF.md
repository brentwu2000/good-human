# P-05 Blind QA Brief — Street Loot & Improvised Weapons
Fresh context. Judge by `docs/07_qa/P05_STREET_LOOT_WEAPON_GATE.md`. Do not read the P-05 engineering notes, data or tests before playing.

Build: `build/windows/GoodHuman.exe` (debug; the debug panel is F1 or five quick taps on the walk timer). Normal play has no fight text: leave 「戰鬥文字/血條」 off.

How weapons turn up: on a walk, searching a bin, the park or the gym corner sometimes finds something the human can fight with. It is left on the ground; bring the human close and use the dog's interact on it. To skip the search luck, the debug panel has 「地上放雨傘」「地上放掃把」「地上放舊啞鈴」 (it appears beside the dog).

Play at least one fight bare-handed and one with each of the three, against more than one kind of opponent.

Ask, without explaining anything first:
- Which one reached furthest? Which was slowest, heaviest? Which defended or countered? When did someone miss because they were standing wrong?
- Did your human behave differently with each? Did any two feel the same?
- Did you want any of them for how your human fights rather than for money? Did that make you want to go home with it — or stay out to find a better one?
- Did losing it (defeat, or it breaking) matter to you?
- What could go in the dog's backpack and what could not, and why?
- Was anything on screen (prompts, the few words near a find, the bag) in the way of the world?

Record: any visible body passing through another or a weapon passing through a body; any hit that landed with nothing visibly touching; whether the camera kept the fight readable; time spent in the bag panel.
