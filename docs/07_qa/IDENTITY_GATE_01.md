# Identity Gate 01
Fresh blind QA.

PASS requires testers to independently express:
1. “This is my dog.”
2. “This is my human / I know what this human is like.”
3. “Something about this human changed because of what I did as the dog.”

Also:
adoption understandable without compatibility %;
owner persists across runs;
at least one habit visibly changes;
at least one memory is referenced later;
appearance is not a dependable strength rating;
no P-04/Sprint05 critical regression.

Ask whether selection felt different from a normal character creator, behavior influenced adoption, the human felt like they chose the dog, owner is recognizable without name, Home feels like returning to the pair, and randomization feels personal rather than disposable.

If owner feels like interchangeable equipment, FAIL.
