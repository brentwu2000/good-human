# Identity Systems

## Dog Selection
Shelter/pet-hospital kennel, third-person observation. Generate ~4–6 candidates from breed/body, appearance, personality, partially visible talent and hidden traits.
Use experiential descriptions (“很愛聞東西”, “精力旺盛”), not a stat spreadsheet.

## Adoption / Owner Matching
After selection briefly enter Dog POV. Human candidates visit. Contextual dog behaviors: WAG, SIT, APPROACH, BARK, FETCH/HOLD_TOY, LICK_HAND, LIE_DOWN, STARE, IGNORE.
Humans have hidden personality/background/preferences/tendencies. Behaviors alter interest; show reactions, never exact compatibility %. Player can try to attract someone but cannot guarantee them.

## Bond
Lightweight internal relationship state (trust/familiarity/shared experience). Prefer behavioral feedback over a grind bar.

## Habits
Repeated dog behavior creates recognizable owner habits: preparing for sprints, anticipating leash tension, resigned trash-search reaction, increased social readiness. Habits express identity and may hook into TRAIN.

## Memories
Store only meaningful events: adoption, first extraction/defeat, territory claim, rival/boss, rare/funny event, major growth. Later dialogue/animation may reference them.

## Pair Identity at Home
Prioritize dog + owner, current habits, recent memory and unresolved Dog Desire. Inventory/stats are secondary.
