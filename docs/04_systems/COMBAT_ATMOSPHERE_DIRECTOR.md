# Combat Atmosphere Director v0.1

Presentation layer only; it does not decide damage or combat outcomes.

## Inputs
Encounter tension, combat phase, owner condition, attack windup/contact, dog action, victory/defeat.

## Camera
Tension push, Dog POV snap, active auto-framing, crisis tightening, resolution hold, release.

## Audio
Explore ambience → gradual duck during tension.
Prioritize dog breathing/growl, leash hardware, footsteps, cloth/body movement and impact.
Introduce restrained low-frequency pulse before/around first strike; avoid generic heroic battle music as the only tension device.

## Character Body Language
Before first hit: stare, posture change, owner hesitation/confidence, approach, footwork.
During combat: readable windup/recovery/fatigue.

## Dog Feedback
Ears/head/posture, breathing, growl/bark. Danger feedback should feel like dog instinct before conventional warning UI.

## World Reaction
Minimum P-03 reactions:
- nearby NPC stops/looks/backs away;
- nearby dog reacts or retreats;
- optional birds scatter on first strong impact.
No complex crowd simulation.

## FX
Small impact accent, dust where contextually valid, hit stop, restrained impulse. No gore requirement.

## Principle
The world should communicate “this is escalating” before the first punch.
