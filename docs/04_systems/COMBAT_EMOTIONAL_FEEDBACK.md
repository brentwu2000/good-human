# Combat Emotional Feedback
Owner should not primarily be read through an HP bar.

HEALTHY: stable/confident.
HURT: heavier breathing, guarded posture, slower recovery.
CRITICAL: unstable footing, fatigue, hesitation.
DOWN: remains in world for resolution beat.

Dog Instinct: head/ears/posture/whine/growl or minimal dog-local danger cue.
Owner→Dog: prototype quick look after useful intervention and post-win acknowledgement/pet. “Good boy!” may be used sparingly.
Defeat: dog remains controllable briefly and can approach downed owner before transition.
Victory: owner recovers, may acknowledge dog, leash relaxes, camera releases.
