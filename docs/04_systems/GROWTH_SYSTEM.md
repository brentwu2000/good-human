# Human Growth System v0.1
Goal: make this human feel shaped by this dog.
Layers: TrainingTags → growth progress → tendencies/stats → perks → observable behavior.
Prototype perks: 被迫晨跑 (RUN), 死都不放牽繩 (STRAIN), 見怪不怪 (COURAGE), 社恐改善中 (SOCIAL), 這狗到底要跑去哪 (RUN+ENDURE), 今天也活下來了 (ENDURE+difficulty), plus 2 hidden mixed-behavior perks.
At least 3 effects must be visible without Stats: less lag/stumble while following a sprinting dog, faster exertion recovery, less hesitation near threats, or improved leash response.
Full branching skill tree is deferred. Appearance must never become a reliable combat-power indicator.
